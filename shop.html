How it is made

The material story matters as much as the shape.

Lumber from local sawmills

Our wood is felled, milled and dried by small sawmills close to home, not pulled from a distributor's rack. We know where each board came from.

Every board chosen by hand

Boards are selected one at a time and matched for grain and color, often from the same tree. Character in the wood is used with intent, not cut away.

Built to be kept

Solid wood, deliberate construction and finishes that can be renewed instead of replaced. Each piece lists its own finish and care.

Before you order

Questions, answered plainly.

How do I receive my order?

Omaha orders are picked up at the studio or delivered locally at no charge. Everywhere else in the United States, we ship. The shipping cost is shown on each piece and again before you pay.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. Card and Apple Pay payments are completed on a secure page hosted by Stripe, then you are returned here. Where Venmo is offered, it is handled by PayPal. Village Woodworking never sees, receives or stores your card number.

Can a piece be personalized?

Pieces that accept engraving or other personalization show a text box when you open them. For anything beyond that, tell us in the order notes or start a commission.

Looking for something made only for you?

The shop holds our smaller work. Tables, built-ins and one-of-one furniture begin with a conversation.
